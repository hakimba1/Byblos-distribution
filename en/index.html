Why Byblos

Named after a merchant port, built as a trading partner

  • Reliable payment on the terms we agree together.
  • A single point of contact who is easy to reach, not an anonymous department.
  • Transparency on sales and customer feedback.
  • Long-term relationships: we are looking for lasting partners.
